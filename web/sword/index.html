Fury

Sword Duel

One arena. One blade. Your phone is the hilt.

  • Swing fast to slash. Strike where their sword isn't guarding.
  • When a side of the screen glows red, point your sword at it to parry. Parried foes take double damage.
  • Parries fill Fury. When full, press Fury for 4 seconds of triple damage that breaks any guard.

Phone: install the Mocon app, open it, then connect. Needs Chrome or Edge.
Mouse: move to aim, flick quickly to slash, Space for Fury, Esc to pause.

Paused

Press Pause on your phone, Esc, or the button below to continue.
