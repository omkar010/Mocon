How to bat
  • Face the screen and hold your phone upright like a bat handle, either side toward the TV. Press Start to take guard.
  • After taking guard you play three practice shots: straight, leg side and off side. Real shots are then placed by comparing them with your practice shots. Early timing pulls the ball to the leg side, late timing to the off side.
  • To hit it in the air, open the face upward or swing big and finish high. Keep the face over the ball and finish low to keep it on the ground.
  • For a right-hander the leg side is the left of the screen and the off side the right; it's the other way round for a left-hander.
  • Mouse: move up to lift the bat, flick down to swing. Keys: arrow keys to swing, Shift to loft. Esc pauses.

Paused

Press Pause on your phone, Esc, or Resume.
