Mocon Games

Your Android phone is the controller. Open the Mocon app, pick a game, and connect over Bluetooth. Every game also plays with a mouse or keyboard.

Playing with your phone

  1. Install and open the Mocon app on your Android phone, and allow Bluetooth.
  2. Open a game here in Chrome or Edge on your computer.
  3. Press Connect phone and choose your phone from the list.
